WHAT IS SAM'S MEDIA CALAMITY?

Sam is an antivirus built to eliminate bugs and restore the internet. But as he travels through a corrupted digital world, something inside him begins to change.

The more Sam discovers about his world, the more he questions what he was created to do — and what he is becoming.

GAMEPLAY

Fast movement, acrobatic combat and exploration across a chaotic digital world.

THE WORLD

Explore a chaotic digital version of the internet filled with strange districts, corrupted systems, enemies and broken technology.

HARMONIA

The first vertical slice takes place in Harmonia — a music-themed district filled with towering musical architecture, floating notes, destroyed buildings and corrupted digital systems.
